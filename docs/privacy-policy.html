Privacy Policy

Be Carrot Social Posting · Effective 8 November 2026

This policy explains how Be Carrot ("we", "us") handles information through Be Carrot Social Posting (the "App"), an internal tool we use to manage social media for the businesses we work with. The App connects to Meta platforms (Facebook and Instagram) only through Meta's official APIs and only for accounts whose owners have given our business access.

Who uses the App

The App is used only by Be Carrot's team, on behalf of our clients. It is not offered to the public, and members of the public do not log in to it.

What information the App accesses

With the permission of the Page, Instagram account or ad account owner, the App may access:

  • Facebook Page and Instagram professional account details: names, IDs and usernames, so content is published to the right account.
  • Content we publish for our clients: videos, images, captions and cover images, and the links and IDs of the resulting posts.
  • Post and account insights: aggregated performance figures such as reach, views and engagement, used for reporting to our clients.
  • Comments and messages on our clients' Pages and Instagram accounts, only where a client asks us to help manage them.
  • Ad account information: campaign settings, budgets, spend and results for ads we run for our clients, including boosted posts.

The App does not ask for, collect or store passwords, payment card details, or private data of people who merely view or interact with our clients' content beyond what Meta's APIs return to the account owner.

How we use it

  • To publish and schedule our clients' posts and reels.
  • To create and manage ads and boosted posts that our clients have approved, within the budgets they set.
  • To report results back to our clients.

We do not sell or rent any information, and we do not use it for any purpose other than the services our clients ask us to provide.

Sharing

Information is shared only with the client whose account it belongs to, and with Meta as needed to carry out the actions above. We may disclose information if required by law.

Storage and security

Access credentials are kept in secure, access-restricted environment settings and are never published. We keep post links, IDs and performance figures in internal trackers for as long as we manage the client's accounts, and delete them on request or when the engagement ends.

Data deletion instructions

To have information the App holds about your Page, Instagram account or ad account deleted:

  1. Email becarrotsocial@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request" and the name of the Page or account.
  2. We will confirm the request and delete the stored information within 30 days.

You can also stop the App's access at any time: in Meta Business Settings, remove Be Carrot's access to your Page, Instagram account or ad account, or in your Facebook settings go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations and remove Be Carrot Social Posting.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top shows the latest version.
