# Privacy policy

**Be Carrot Social Posting** · effective 8 November 2026

This app is an internal tool used by the Be Carrot team to manage the Facebook Pages, Instagram accounts and ad
accounts we handle for our clients. It is not offered to the public, and members of the public do not log in to it.
It connects to Facebook and Instagram only through Meta's official APIs, and only for accounts whose owners have
given our business access.

## What the app accesses

- **Pages and Instagram professional accounts** our business has been given access to: names, IDs and usernames,
  so content goes to the right account.
- **Content we publish for clients**: videos, captions and cover images that the account owner has approved, and the
  link and ID of each published post.
- **Insights**: aggregated performance figures such as reach, views and engagement, for reporting to our clients.
- **Comments and messages** on our clients' accounts, only where a client asks us to help manage them.
- **Ad accounts** our business has been given access to: campaign settings, budgets, spend and results for ads and
  boosted posts that the client has approved.

It does not collect, store or share personal data about the people who view or follow these accounts beyond what
Meta's APIs return to the account owner, and it never asks for passwords or payment card details.

## How it is used

- To publish our clients' posts and reels.
- To create and manage ads and boosted posts that our clients have approved, within the budgets they set.
- To report results back to our clients.

We do not sell or rent any information or use it for anything other than the services our clients ask for. It is
shared only with the client whose account it belongs to, and with Meta as needed to carry out these actions.

## Storage

Access tokens are stored privately and are never published in this repository. Post links, IDs and performance
figures are kept in internal trackers for as long as we manage the client's accounts, and deleted on request or when
the engagement ends.

## Data deletion

To ask a question about this app or to request removal of any data, open an issue in this repository or email
becarrotsocial@gmail.com with the name of the Page or account. We delete the stored information within 30 days.

You can also stop the app's access at any time: in Meta Business Settings, remove Be Carrot's access to your Page,
Instagram account or ad account, or in Facebook go to *Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations* and
remove **Be Carrot Social Posting**.
