# Privacy policy

This app is an internal tool used by our team to publish videos to the Facebook Pages and Instagram
accounts we manage for our clients.

- The app only accesses Pages and Instagram accounts that our business has been given access to.
- It uses that access to publish videos, captions and cover images that the account owner has approved,
  and to read back the link of each published post.
- It does not collect, store or share personal data about the people who view or follow these accounts.
- Access tokens are stored privately and are never published in this repository.

To ask a question about this app or to request removal of any data, open an issue in this repository.
