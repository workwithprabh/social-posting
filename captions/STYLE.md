# Caption style per client

Learned from each account's last 10 Instagram posts (read 7 Nov 2026 with `scripts/recent_captions.py`).
Two styles show up: the older posts (Oct) are short and mostly English with about 5 hashtags; the newest
reels (Nov, written by the team in ChatGPT and posted through this repo) are longer Hinglish with emoji bullets.
**Follow the newest style** unless the client's section says otherwise. Only use facts said in the reel.

## Shape of a reel caption (Arav, Dee Emm Mahindra, Dee Emm Hyundai, M R Hyundai)

1. **Hook line** in Hinglish, a question or myth the reel answers, ending in 1-2 emojis. `CNG car hai? Petrol wali service se kaam nahi chalega! ⛽🚫`
2. **One or two short lines** that set up the problem, in the presenter's own words where possible.
3. **Emoji bullet list** of the facts from the reel (✅ for checks and features, ⚡ for specs, ❌ or ⚠️ for risks, 👇 to lead into the list).
4. **Takeaway line**, often with 💯 / 🔥 / 💰.
5. **Call to action** naming the dealership: `Khud feel karna hai? Test drive book kijiye DEE EMM MAHINDRA pe 🚗`
6. **Contact block** (exact lines below).
7. **Engagement line**: save it, send it to a friend, or answer in the comments. `Ye reel us dost ko bhejo jo CNG car chalata hai 😉`
8. **Hashtags**: 8 to 15. The client tag first, then brand, model, topic, city.

Festival posts: a feed image gets a one-liner (`Happy Dhanteras 👣🙏`). A festival **reel** gets a warm Hinglish
greeting of 4-6 short lines that echoes the reel, then the contact block and festival hashtags
(MR Hyundai's Dhanteras reel is the model: `Aap sabhi ko aur aapke parivaar ko sukh, samriddhi aur khushiyon se bhara Dhanteras mubarak ho! 🪔`).
