# Caption style per client

No bold or other markdown in captions (user, 9 Nov 2026): Instagram and Facebook show `**` and `__` as-is. Plain text
and emojis only, no trailing spaces at line ends.

Learned from each account's last 10 Instagram posts (read 7 Nov 2026 with `scripts/recent_captions.py`).
Two styles show up: the older posts (Oct) are short and mostly English with about 5 hashtags; the newest
reels (Nov, written by the team in ChatGPT and posted through this repo) are longer Hinglish with emoji bullets.
Follow the newest style unless the client's section says otherwise. Only use facts said in the reel.

## Shape of a reel caption (Arav, Dee Emm Mahindra, Dee Emm Hyundai, M R Hyundai)

**Keep captions short (user, 15 Nov 2026: "the current ones are a bit lengthy").** About 60-90 words in all, contact
block included: a hook line, the 2-3 key facts from the reel (one line or a short emoji list), one call to action, the
contact block, at most one engagement line, and about 5 hashtags. Leave out set-up lines, repeated takeaways and
long tag lists. Examples (15 Nov 2026): AM-2610-07, DEH-2610-06, MRH-2611-03, DEM-2611-02 in the Captions tab.
The longer shape below is still the order to follow; use only the parts that fit.
**Shorter still (user, 21 Nov 2026: "make the captions a bit short"):** aim for about 50-60 words with the contact
block: hook line, at most 3 one-line facts, the comment call to action (or a share line), contact block, 5 hashtags.
No set-up paragraph and no separate takeaway line. Examples: AM-2611-04 to AM-2611-07 (Captions tab).

**Comment-for-reply call to action (user, 18 Nov 2026).** Boosts stay on engagement, which brings likes but almost no
comments or profile visits, so reels that should bring enquiries (offers, EMI, pre-owned/Promise, stock lists, savings
calculators) get one line asking for a keyword comment with a promised reply, placed just before the contact block,
in the client's language and true to the reel: e.g. "Comment OFFER and we'll send you the offer on your model.",
"Comment EMI aur hum aapke budget ka EMI plan DM karenge 👇". The team must reply to those comments (or DM) the same day.
Examples: CH-2611-03, CH-2611-05, DEM-2611-04, AM-2611-02 (Captions tab).


1. **Hook line** in Hinglish, a question or myth the reel answers, ending in 1-2 emojis. `CNG car hai? Petrol wali service se kaam nahi chalega! ⛽🚫`
2. **One or two short lines** that set up the problem, in the presenter's own words where possible.
3. **Emoji bullet list** of the facts from the reel (✅ for checks and features, ⚡ for specs, ❌ or ⚠️ for risks, 👇 to lead into the list).
4. **Takeaway line**, often with 💯 / 🔥 / 💰.
5. **Call to action** naming the dealership: `Khud feel karna hai? Test drive book kijiye DEE EMM MAHINDRA pe 🚗`
6. **Contact block** (exact lines below).
7. **Engagement line**: save it, send it to a friend, or answer in the comments. `Ye reel us dost ko bhejo jo CNG car chalata hai 😉`
8. **Hashtags**: about 5 (was 8 to 15 before 15 Nov 2026). The client tag first, then brand, model, topic, city.

Festival posts: a feed image gets a one-liner (`Happy Dhanteras 👣🙏`). A festival **reel** gets a warm Hinglish
greeting of 4-6 short lines that echoes the reel, then the contact block and festival hashtags
(MR Hyundai's Dhanteras reel is the model: `Aap sabhi ko aur aapke parivaar ko sukh, samriddhi aur khushiyon se bhara Dhanteras mubarak ho! 🪔`).
