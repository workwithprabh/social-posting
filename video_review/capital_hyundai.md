# Capital Hyundai video review log

Running log of editor reviews of Capital Hyundai reels before posting. Timestamps are mm:ss in the Drive file.

## What to flag (user, 15 Nov 2026)

Focus on:
- **Spelling and grammar** in subtitles and on-screen text (model names: Creta, Venue, BAYON, Verna, Alcazar,
  Exter, i20, IONIQ 5).
- **Facts:** prices, offers and validity dates, specs, variant-only features, claims. Check them against
  hyundai.com/in, press releases or the client. Prices and offers need "T&C apply".
- **Dealer and brand:** no other dealer's or brand's name or logo on screen.
- **Capital Hyundai logo:** on screen throughout, and visible (not lost on white backgrounds).

Ignore: missing subtitles, text in Instagram's caption or button area (bottom 20% or right edge), audio levels,
trims and pacing, transitions, and number plates.

How a reel is checked: faster-whisper transcript (English and Hindi passes), frames at 1 fps plus OCR at 4 fps,
zoomed frames for signs and badges, facts against hyundai.com/in, press reports and Capital Hyundai's Google Business
Profile.

   The correct number is **99589 94275** (user, 15 Nov 2026), as on the Nov reels' card. Use the Nov card file
