## 2026-11-20 · Topic 1: fog driving (written by Claude for the 21 Nov shoot)
- Changed angle: "hazard lights while driving = galti" is contested. Cyberabad police and Team-BHP say hazards
  only when stopped, but the UP CM's Dec 2025 fog directive and Noida's advisory tell drivers to keep emergency
  indicators on. MR Hyundai is in Ghaziabad (UP), so the reel must not call it a mistake. New angle: high beam
  in fog, which every advisory agrees on (low beam + fog lamps, high beam reflects off fog).
- Hazards line kept only for the case all sources agree on: pull over in a safe spot, then hazards on.
- Fog lamps: not on every variant, so "agar aapki Hyundai mein fog lamps hain". Check the car being filmed.
- Score (final): 9/10 (hook 9, idea 8, accuracy 9, flow 8, voice 9, CTA 9, shootability 8)
- User fix: "high beam maarta hai" -> "high beam use karta hai"; read-through also fixed the double "aur" and
  made the address one form (aap) throughout.
- Final script:

  Fog mein aage kuch dikh nahi raha, toh high beam on kar di?
  Yahi sabse badi galti hai!

  High beam ki roshni fog se takra ke wapas aati hai. Saamne ek safed deewar si ban jaati hai, jisse dikhna aur bhi kam ho jaata hai.

  Isliye fog mein headlights hamesha low beam pe rakhiye. Aur agar aapki Hyundai mein fog lamps hain, toh unhe bhi on kar dijiye.

  Speed kam rakhiye, aage wali gaadi se doori zyada, aur overtake bilkul nahi.
  Aur agar fog itna ghana hai ki kuch dikh hi nahi raha, toh gaadi kisi safe jagah pe side mein rokiye, phir hazard lights on kijiye.

  Lights ya fog lamps theek se kaam nahi kar rahe? MR Hyundai, Shalimar Garden aaiye, hum check kar denge. Aur ye video us dost ko bhejiye jo fog mein bhi high beam use karta hai!

## 2026-11-20 · Topic 2: windshield fogging inside (written by Claude for the 21 Nov shoot)
- Score: 9/10 (hook 9, idea 9, accuracy 8, flow 8, voice 9, CTA 9, shootability 9)
- Sources: Hyundai owner's manuals (2025 Palisade LX2, Tucson NX4, NH2 2026): defog inside = A/C on, fresh-air
  mode, defrost mode, fan on; rear defroster heats the conductors on the glass, turns off after about 20 min;
  never use sharp instruments or abrasive cleaners on the inside of the rear glass. Not India-model pages:
  check the button layout on the car being filmed; rear defogger varies by variant.
- User fix: many cars have no defrost button (manual AC uses a mode knob with a windshield position); script
  now says "windshield wala sign, knob pe ho ya button pe"; rear defogger line starts with "agar".
- User: middle was confusing. Refined into 3 numbered steps ("ek, do, teen"), fresh air explained as "bahar ki
  hawa wala mode", rear glass cut to one line, scratch warning moved to the caption. About 30 s, 9/10 (flow 9).
- Final script:

  Subah gaadi start ki, aur sheesha andar se dhundhla?
  Kapde se ponchna band kijiye! Dhundh thodi der mein wapas aa jaayegi.

  Bas teen kaam kijiye.
  Ek, AC ki hawa windshield ki taraf kar dijiye. Knob ho ya button, windshield wala sign chuniye.
  Do, AC on aur fan thoda tez kijiye. Haan, thand mein bhi AC, kyunki AC hawa ki nami sukha deta hai.

  Bas, kuch hi der mein sheesha saaf.

  Aur peeche ke sheeshe ke liye, agar aapki gaadi mein rear defogger hai, toh bas uska button daba dijiye.

  before. RC has a "Month/Year of Manufacture" field; Form 21 (sale certificate) carries the date of manufacture.

  no resale numbers.

  Car December mein lein ya January mein?
  Ye sawaal har saal aata hai, toh aaj seedha jawab.

  Aur December mein lein, toh ek kaam zaroor kijiye: booking se pehle gaadi ka manufacturing month pooch lijiye. Ye RC aur gaadi ke sale papers mein bhi likha hota hai.

## 2026-11-21 · Topic 4: four warning lights you must never ignore (written by Claude)
- Score: 9/10 (hook 9, idea 9, accuracy 9, flow 8, voice 9, CTA 9, shootability 9)
- Source: Hyundai India owner's manual, i20 N Line (hyundai.com/in), pages 5-9 to 5-13 and 9-7/9-8: oil pressure,
  charging system and brake fluid lights = "Drive carefully to the nearest safe location and stop your
  vehicle"; overheating = "Pull off the road and stop as soon as it is safe to do so"; "NEVER remove the
  radiator cap ... while the engine and radiator are hot"; brake light with parking brake released = low brake
  fluid; lights glow for about 3 s at ignition ON (oil and charging stay on until the engine starts).
- Variants: some cars have a temperature gauge, others a high-temperature light, so the script names both.
- Final script:

  Dashboard pe ye 4 lights dikhein, toh ignore mat kijiye. Safe jagah dekh ke gaadi rokiye!

  Pehli, engine oil wali light, jo oil ki kuppi jaisi dikhti hai. Iska matlab oil pressure kam hai. Gaadi rokiye, engine band kijiye, aur oil check kijiye.

  Doosri, engine temperature. Temperature ki sui upar chali jaaye ya temperature wali light jale, toh engine garam ho raha hai. Gaadi side mein rokiye, aur garam engine mein radiator ka dhakkan bilkul mat kholiye.

  Teesri, battery wali light. Iska matlab charging system mein dikkat hai. Gaadi rokiye aur jaldi check karwaiye.

  Chauthi, brake wali light. Handbrake hata diya, phir bhi ye light jal rahi hai, toh brake fluid kam ho sakta hai. Gaadi rokiye, aur brake theek se kaam na karein toh gaadi mat chalaiye.

  Haan, gaadi on karte waqt ye lights kuch second jalke band ho jaati hain, wo normal hai. Problem tab hai jab ye chalte waqt jalein.

  In mein se koi light jali? MR Hyundai, Shalimar Garden aaiye, hum check kar denge. Aur ye video save kar lijiye, kabhi bhi kaam aa sakta hai!
